Manifesto

Art matters.

We believe art is not a luxury. It is a necessity.

Art has the power to move us, challenge us, heal us and, ultimately, elevate our consciousness. It allows us to imagine beyond what exists and to see the world, and ourselves, with greater depth, empathy and possibility.

We believe in a world where art is not merely consumed as leisure or pastime, but recognised as a force that can shape culture, communities and the future.

That is why we exist.

We are building a home for artists and ideas, a space where cinema, music and every other form of artistic expression can coexist, evolve and find their audience.

We discover, nurture, produce and build with artists who have something meaningful to say. We believe creative freedom and commercial sustainability do not have to be opposites. Art can have purpose, reach and value, and still remain art.

Our ambition is simple, yet expansive: to build an organisation for artists the way great organisations have been built for their communities of believers.

Because we believe that when art evolves, people evolve. And when consciousness rises, the world changes.

About

Uncombed Buddha means finding peace in chaos — a reality of life we try to depict through cinema, sound, and whatever form the work asks for. Founded in 2018 in Jabalpur by Harsh Agarwal and Aditya Grover, we've grown from a small independent outfit into a production house whose work travels to festivals across the world.

We call ourselves Daydreamers — we work every stage of a project, from first idea through to release, across film, music, and the other forms the work takes. Our films have premiered at Locarno, Busan, Rotterdam and Sundance, and screened at festivals worldwide, told in languages and dialects that rarely reach a screen.
